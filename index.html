System requirements

Minimal

  • ОС: Windows 12
  • CPU: Intel Core i9 14900KS / AMD Ryzen 9 9950x
  • DRAM: 64 GB DDR5 6000
  • GPU: NVIDIA RTX 5090 32 GB / RX 9070 XT 24 GB
  • DirectX: Version 12
  • Storage: 1 TB (SSD)

Recommended

  • ОС: Windows 15
  • CPU: AMD Ryzen Threadripper 9995WX / Intel Xeon W9-3595X
  • DRAM: 256 GB DDR6 9000
  • GPU: NVIDIA Blackwell B200 192 VRAM
  • DirectX: Version 12
  • Storage: 10 TB (Recommended SSD)
