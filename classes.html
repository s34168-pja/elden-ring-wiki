Starting Character Classes

Choose your origin to begin exploration across the Lands Between

In Elden Ring, your chosen Starting Class determines your initial attributes, allocation of combat statistics, and starting equipment. While all classes can freely transform into any build later in the game through attribute leveling, your origin provides a foundational playstyle optimized for early-game survival.

Strategic Build Advice

If you prefer sweeping physical attacks, the Hero provides the highest raw physical scaling properties. For swift evasion combined with ranged precision, the Samurai stands as an exceptional balanced hybrid. Players tracking mystical forces should employ the Astrologer to secure immediate access to powerful projectile sorceries.
